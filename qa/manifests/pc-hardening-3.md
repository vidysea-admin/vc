K1 scope: the check is on the student verifier (chat, profile, voice sessions, i.e. every student JWT route); the counsellor JWT verifier in server.ts is a different identity space and is untouched. A partner added within the 60 s registry cache window is not seen until the window ends; an unreadable partner list refuses the student token with the same uniform 401 (a registry outage is a student-route outage by design: fail closed).
