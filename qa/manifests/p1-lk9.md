## Open risks
1. Behaviour change vs D-093 4A as originally worded ("an unconsented minor still gets answers"): such a student now gets the fixed consent reply and NO answers until a guardian-consent claim is on the token. This is the brief's C1 and is fail closed, but it is a product change for under-18 and unknown-age students; the old test that pinned the old behaviour was replaced, and the chat.ts header comment states the new rule. Whether D-093 4A needs a superseding DECISIONS entry is the orchestrator's call (maker never edits docs/DECISIONS.md).
2. C1 side effects kept on purpose: an unconsented minor's ordinary turn is still rate limited, still marks the thread `textStored:false`, records no usage count and no gap count, has no idempotent replay (a retry gets the same fixed reply), and the LK5 safety-screen model no longer runs for them (only the offline recall-only `crisisGuard` does). Wording of `GUARDIAN_CONSENT_REPLY` is English only and not human-vetted (student-facing text); the Hindi reply is not provided.
3. C2: a fact is no longer visible on `GET .../chat/profile` in the same instant as the reply (a client that fetches the profile immediately after the turn can miss it for a short moment). A student whose erasure begins during extraction now gets a 200 for the turn (previously 409 `student_fenced` from the extraction block) and one `chat_fact_extraction_fenced` log line; the fact write is still refused by the fenced writer (test-proved). A process stop between the reply and the extraction loses that turn's proposals (acceptable: facts are proposals only). A hung extractor leaks one pending promise per turn until its own timeout (the default extractor has one; an injected one may not).
4. The 8 `settle()` calls added to existing tests wait 8 event-loop turns; they are timing-based, not event-based. The never-resolving and pending tests use real 3 s guards. Under heavy load a settle could in theory be too short; none failed in the runs recorded above.
5. The checker should confirm C1 against `chat.ts:637` (`consentOk`) and that `level` (computed on the full original text, before truncation) is what gates the crisis exception, including the over-long crisis case (textless variant, no provider call).
