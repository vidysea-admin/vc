**Build**
- Thread and turn routes, idempotent.
- **Redacted persistence (D-093 6A).** Names, phone numbers and emails in student turns are replaced by placeholders before storage. The live turn may still go to the model.
- **Student JWT, config-driven (D-093 item 11; Pathlynks hasn't given the format yet).** Issuer, audience and JWKS URL or HS secret come from env. Tests use synthetic tokens. Student routes reject counsellor and admin tokens.
- **Under-18 rule (D-093 4A).** Pathlynks supplies a guardian-consent claim. A minor without it still gets answers, but nothing personal persists: the thread stores only a text-free marker.
- **Profile routes.** The Pathlynks-supplied profile, plus student confirm/correct of chat facts.
- **Limits.** 30 requests per minute per student, with per-tenant overrides.
- **Tenants (A6).** Two-tenant isolation plus the sandbox tenant.
- **Logs.** The crisis flag log and gap counts go in the virtual_counsellor collections.
- **Erasure and export** cover threads, facts and usage counters.
- **Flag.** Everything sits behind a default-off flag, and no route is enabled without the A20 gate.
- **Database.** Tests use the fake DB (packages/db/tests/fakes.ts). Never connect to Mongo.
