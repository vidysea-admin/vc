## 4. Risks and gates
- **Voice cannot meet pre-send hard rules.** Gemini Live speaks before text guards run. B2 citations, B1 no-quote and crisis are monitored, not enforced, in voice: a spoken breach cannot be unsaid. Hence a B6 amendment, A9b (the audit demonstrably fires), A10 with crisis set and max-audio bound (a Q5 limit), and voice disabled until A10 passes (A20). D-086 keeps Gemini Live with persisted transcripts.
- **Production data basis: approved (D-086).** Real minors' text and voice go to Gemini in production, and anonymous mode processes minors' text with no sign-in. The B13 amendment in U0 records provider terms, retention and DPDP basis. Until it is signed, G-PROD blocks real-user exposure, the public anonymous route and partner publication.
- **Crisis liability is minimised, not removed (D-087).** The proposal states zero liability is not achievable. Open outside P1: the CEO Nights decision (human alert), whether minors are served before counsel clears DPDP, and who owns the clinical-safety policy; Tele-MANAS adolescent and language coverage is unverified (test-call before launch). P1 ships the disclosed Tele-MANAS hand-off and complete flag logs. Anonymous mode keeps no log by design, so a crisis there is not recorded (Q7).
- **KnowledgeBase API fit (D-087):** the KB is used as it is. Risks: the response fields for licence marker (G1) and no-log behaviour (G3) are unverified until U2; "worldwide" answers are only as wide as KB content, so most countries show as gaps, not as authored pages; KB outage returns the fixed no-answer text. A section 7 gap becomes a U1 KB change only when confirmed.
- **Profile quality:** chat-extracted facts can be wrong; they are used only after the student confirms (A16b). Pathlynks-supplied profiles are trusted as supplied.
- **Consent is outside VC (D-086):** Pathlynks-authenticated = consented; VC adds no consent-claim verification, so a wrong assumption at Pathlynks is not caught by VC. Existing consent/purpose records (`packages/db/src/consent.ts`) need a contract decision on whether they remain (U0).
- **Coach-first (D-087):** the line between coaching and drafting is judged by A21(a) fixtures and the head-to-head; drafting stays out.
- **Content licence:** WhatsApp and seminar material may need restrictions (Q9).
- **Mock vs real:** mock tests prove plumbing only; model behaviour is proved by REAL runs.
- **Environment:** full C: OOMs the suite (ENVIRONMENTAL). Build in isolated worktrees, never in dirty `D:\vc`. No push, deploy, partner exposure or paid run without Umesh's gate.
