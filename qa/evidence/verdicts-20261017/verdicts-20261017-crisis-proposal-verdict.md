## Defects (all non-blocking). Exact fix for each.
- W1 Overstatement. Item 5: "It is built mostly for adults". Citizen Matters [65] says about 70% of callers are 18-45, but also that Tele MANAS "emphasises being inclusive of ... children, adolescents" and describes a 17-year-old caller. The report's "built for adults" comes from a practitioner lens, not the source. - W2 Wrong-jurisdiction support. "Excluding them is allowed [38]" (Decision 2). [38] is Newsom's veto message on California AB 1064, saying a broad bill "may unintentionally lead to a total ban on the use of these products by minors". It does not show Indian law lets a counselling company exclude minors. Fix: "Excluding under-18s looks like a product choice (no Indian rule found requiring us to serve them; counsel to confirm), but students disclose anyway [42]." Drop [38] or label it "US example".
- W3 Sukdeb Saha scope not stated. Item 6 and the Liability section cite [44] for a "written, drilled escalation plan" and training twice a year. The guidelines (para 35-36) bind "all educational institutions ... coaching institutes ... hostels", not a private AI study-abroad company. Fix: add "The Supreme Court's Sukdeb Saha rules bind schools and coaching centres, not us directly; we use them as the benchmark." Cite [61] for twice-yearly training, since the proposal's "staff training twice a year [44]" is only in para 35 of the judgment text.
- W4 Weak support for the guardian-override rules. Item 7 cites [51] and [52]. MHCA s.23 governs "health professionals providing care or treatment to a person with mental illness", and its exception (e) is "release only such information as is necessary to prevent threat to life" without saying whose life; no exception expressly covers a person's own self-harm. MHCA s.15 only says the guardian is the nominated representative unless the Board orders otherwise; it says nothing about withholding information from a possibly harmful parent. The proposal already says "thin evidence; counsel to confirm". Fix: change the cites to "[51] by analogy, [52] background only", and add MHCA s.23 and s.15 to the lawyer list ("do these apply to a non-clinical chatbot at all").
- W5 Allegations read as fact. "The Problem": "If the bot notices and nothing happens, that is the worst position, morally and legally [32][42]." [32] and [42] are allegations in a complaint (Bloomberg: "the complaint said"; Wikipedia: "According to the filing"). Fix: "... the worst position; US suits allege exactly this [32][42] (allegations, not findings)."
- W6 Off-point cites. Item 1 "[10]" is MHCA s.87, admission of minors, which does not support "never diagnoses or calls itself therapy"; use [24] only.
